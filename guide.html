💰Money 11 tips

Cash is tight early on. Protect what you have and pick the right side hustles.

  1. Stash your cash

    Cash in your pocket is gone when you die or get busted. Drop it in the floorboard stash in Jason's bedroom; it takes deposits any time.

    Source: Outputlag · Red Bull · U7BUY

  2. Check the mailbox

    Pay from missions and from Brian and Donnie often lands in the mailbox outside Jason's house. Look every time you leave home.

    Source: Outputlag

  3. Open a BuckMe account

    The Buck Me mission unlocks your BuckMe bank account for ATMs. Shops only take up to $999 in pocket cash; bigger buys come out of BuckMe.

    Guides differ on when: about 1-2 hours in, or halfway through Chapter 1.

    Source: Outputlag · U7BUY

  4. Treasure diving with Kenton

    Visit Kenton in Key Lento to unlock diving. Wrecks show as purple chest icons on the map.

    One guide says Kenton pays on the spot (about $1,400 for the first wreck), another says he only pays after selling the loot.

    Source: VICE · Outputlag

  5. Crack the safe at Three Sheets Liquor

    At the exotic animals front in Key Lento, go round Three Sheets Liquor, climb through the hole in the wall and crack the back-room safe. No code needed.

    Source: VICE

  6. Don't miss Shirley's Rum

    This errand is missable. Talk to the parrot, and grab about $1,300 from the white wall vent by the yellow generator.

    Source: Outputlag

  7. Watch the hourglass

    Errands with an hourglass on their map icon expire. Main missions wait for you.

    Source: Outputlag

  8. Sell stolen cars

    Any Pay 'n' Spray buys stolen cars in Chapter 1, with a daily limit. From Chapter 2, Moses buys them with no daily limit.

    Source: VICE · U7BUY

  9. Use the snap gun

    A snap gun opens many back doors without setting off the alarm.

    Source: Red Bull

  10. Mind your stash bags

    Each character carries two bags. Vans and trucks hold up to eight in the trunk, motorcycles none. Die with full bags and you lose them.

    Source: Red Bull

  11. Spend before Chapter 2

    Moving into Chapter 2 wipes your pocket cash, the floor stash and your main BuckMe balance. Spend it on gear first.

    Source: Outputlag · U7BUY

🔫Weapons & combat 9 tips

Keep the guns you paid for and fight smart, not loud.

  1. Buy the guns you want to keep

    Only guns bought at Ammu-Nation come back after you die. Guns dropped by enemies are lost. Your owned guns are kept in the trunk of the Ganado.

    Source: Outputlag · Red Bull

  2. Holster in crowds

    Put your gun away (L1 / LB) before walking into a crowd. People who see a drawn gun call the police.

    Source: Outputlag

  3. Get a suppressor early

    Buy one at the Ammu-Nation in Key Lento right after the tutorial. A silenced gun doesn't alert nearby enemies or police.

    Source: Red Bull

  4. Don't skip the long gun

    In Gear Up, buy the long gun at Chuck's Tackle & Shot in Key Lento. Skipping it can stall the Feng Shui call.

    Source: Outputlag

  5. Two plus two

    You carry two sidearms and two long guns. The rest waits in the trunk.

    Source: Outputlag

  6. Aim for head or heart

    Head and heart hits kill, limb hits injure. A red hit marker means a kill, yellow means injured.

    Source: Red Bull

  7. Use Focus Mode in fights

    Click both sticks with a gun out to slow time for a moment. Practising at the shooting range improves Focus.

    Source: Red Bull · U7BUY

  8. Go first person with a sight

    With a red dot sight, click the right stick to aim in first person. Iron sights can't.

    Source: Red Bull

  9. Shoot the tyres

    Shoot out one or two tyres and pursuers spin out.

    Source: Red Bull

🚗Cars & driving 9 tips

Scan before you steal, and only keep what you can register.

  1. Scan with WAiNK

    The WAiNK app shows a car's tracker, sell value, locks, alarm and registration cost before you take it.

    Source: Red Bull · Outputlag · U7BUY

  2. Skip cars with trackers

    An advanced tracker pings the police if you drive far. Moses won't buy a car with a tracker.

    Source: Red Bull · Outputlag

  3. Keyless cars need a cloner

    Most cars can be hotwired. High-end keyless cars need a key cloner.

    Source: Red Bull · U7BUY

  4. Register cars to keep them

    Register a car at Pay 'n' Spray; the worse the car, the cheaper the fee. Registered cars can be called with the Scooter Bros app.

    Source: Red Bull

  5. Stolen cars don't stay

    A stolen car disappears when you die, get busted or leave it, even parked outside Jason's house. Jason's Ganado is the only car you keep until Chapter 2.

    Source: Outputlag

  6. Pick the Ganado colour carefully

    The respray in Just a Scratch is the only free one.

    Source: Outputlag

  7. Don't wreck what you sell

    Damage lowers the resale value.

    Source: U7BUY

  8. Buy a garage later

    Once Jason and Lucia leave Key Lento you can buy garages (just under a dozen) and store cars there for free.

    Source: Red Bull · Outputlag

  9. Leave your neighbours' cars

    Cars in Jason's own neighbourhood can't be stolen.

    Source: Outputlag

🚨Police & wanted level 8 tips

The police work from what they know about you. Give them less to go on.

  1. Know what they know

    Icons under the wanted stars show whether officers know your outfit, face or car.

    Source: Outputlag

  2. Change your look

    Keep a spare outfit in the trunk (up to five) and wear a hat and bandana during a crime. Take them off afterwards.

    Source: Outputlag · Red Bull

  3. No witness, no cops

    Police only come when someone reports the crime.

    Source: Outputlag

  4. Cut the alarm, wipe the tape

    In a store robbery the alarm panel and the security footage are in the back room. Cut the alarm and destroy the footage.

    Source: Outputlag · Red Bull

  5. Break line of sight

    Lose them, leave the search area and stay away for a while. Police may keep watching an area where you were identified.

    Source: Outputlag · U7BUY

  6. Don't shoot cops

    Killing officers raises your heat and pushes your profile toward Violent. Hiding works better.

    Source: Outputlag

  7. Make the chopper leave

    A police helicopter retreats after taking enough hits; the pilot says so over the radio.

    Source: Red Bull

  8. Work at night

    A quiet street at night makes a job easier.

    Source: Red Bull

🎬Missions & story 7 tips

Some things can only be done once. Don't rush past them.

  1. Play the Prologue in one go

    You can't save during the Prologue.

    Source: Outputlag

  2. Finish errands before Tourist Trap

    Tourist Trap starts back-to-back story missions to the end of Chapter 1 with no free roam.

    Source: Outputlag

  3. Don't miss Lucia's balcony

    Once Lucia lives at the house, check the kitchen in the morning and accept the optional task. It can't be done later.

    Source: Outputlag

  4. Breakfast Run unlocks switching

    After Breakfast Run you can switch freely between Jason and Lucia.

    Source: Outputlag

  5. Your first big choice

    In Hey Arnold! you decide to kill or spare Arnold. It weighs on your criminal profile.

    Source: Outputlag

  6. Bigger jobs come later

    Chapter 3 gives dye pack jammers for banks and loan stores; bank vaults open late in Chapter 4.

    Source: Red Bull · U7BUY

  7. Rob as a duo

    Invite your partner with Triangle / Y on the job's map icon.

    Source: Red Bull

🎮Controls & hidden mechanics 9 tips

Small settings and tricks most players never find.

  1. Save manually

    Pause > Game > Save Game. There are 15 slots, autosave only runs after missions, and missions can't be replayed.

    Source: Outputlag

  2. Focus Mode spots loot

    Click L3 + R3: enemies light up red, interactables yellow, cash green. Doors show white (enterable) or red (alarmed), and notes with safe codes show through walls.

    Source: Red Bull · Outputlag

  3. Better camera settings

    Motion Blur to 0, Look and Aim Sensitivity from 7 to 9 or 10, and Movement Speed to Run.

    Source: Outputlag

  4. Read the map colours

    Dark blue buildings can be entered, light blue is scenery. Check back doors on the minimap for hidden rooms.

    Source: Red Bull · U7BUY

  5. Phone = clock

    Press up on the D-pad to see the time. Shops close at night; sleep at Jason's house to skip ahead.

    Source: Red Bull

  6. No fast travel

    Call Paradise Taxi, take buses and trains, or switch characters, which is the cheapest way to travel.

    Source: Outputlag · Red Bull

  7. Skip a RydeMe ride

    Pay about $100 extra to skip the trip.

    Source: Red Bull · Outputlag

  8. Switch characters fast

    Hold right on the D-pad and press R1 / RB.

    Source: Outputlag

  9. Pass a bag

    Give a stash bag to your partner: right on the D-pad, then Circle / B.

    Source: Red Bull

💪Jason & Lucia stats 9 tips

Train a little every day and keep your criminal profile in check.

  1. Three stats

    Strength (weights) raises max health, Cardio (running, biking, kayak, swimming) raises stamina, Focus (fishing, pool, hoops, shooting range) lasts longer in combat.

    Source: Red Bull · U7BUY · Outputlag

  2. Daily caps

    Strength and Cardio have a daily limit, Focus doesn't.

    Source: Outputlag

  3. Gym technique

    Press both triggers quickly and release slowly. Stop when the fitness ring closes.

    Source: Red Bull

  4. Drink the shake

    A protein shake from the fridge halves recovery time.

    Source: Outputlag · Red Bull

  5. Keep training

    Health and stamina gains fade if you stop. Focus stays.

    Source: Red Bull

  6. Buffs from daily life

    Eating, sleeping and showering give short buffs: pink heart (health), blue bolt (stamina), purple eye (Focus). Feeding Dolores the snake gives a small Focus buff.

    Source: Red Bull

  7. Fix the house

    Four free house repairs (steps, roof, railing, wall) improve Jason and Lucia's relationship.

    Source: Outputlag

  8. Watch your profile

    Five tiers: Professional, Hardened, Violent, Psycho and Irredeemable. At Psycho you can't surrender, and Irredeemable is permanent.

    Source: Red Bull · Outputlag

  9. Stay Professional for Focus

    At Professional, takedowns and non-lethal play fill Focus. At Violent and Psycho, headshots do.

    Source: Outputlag · Red Bull
